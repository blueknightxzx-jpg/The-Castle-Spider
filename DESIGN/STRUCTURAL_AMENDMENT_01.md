# Structural Amendment 01
## HALLWAY, CONTROLS, CHARACTER, AND CASTLE ALIGNMENT

Applied after v0.3 review and before v0.4.

### Decisions
1. The game's primary traversal is a side-view hallway.
2. Player movement is strictly horizontal.
3. A/D and Left/Right are the movement controls.
4. Shift remains sprint.
5. W/S and Up/Down are removed from movement.
6. E is reserved as the interaction key, but interaction gameplay is not implemented until v0.4.
7. The default player is a neutral character.
8. The knight becomes a future skin/design rather than the hard-coded player.
9. Castle architecture is rebuilt around a fixed alignment grid to eliminate messy overlaps and inconsistent spacing.

### Reason
These changes improve readability, make the hallway traversal intentional, and create a cleaner foundation for future interaction, skins and horror systems.

### Re-lock
This amendment is part of the locked master roadmap until explicitly revised again.
