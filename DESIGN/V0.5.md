# The Castle Spider — v0.5
## SPIDER FOUNDATION

v0.5 introduces the first real spider entity without activating hunting or damage.

### Current states
**Dormant**
- Spider remains still.
- No chase behavior.
- Low visual emphasis.

**Listening**
- Triggered by nearby player movement.
- Sprinting can alert the spider from farther away.
- Spider gets a stronger visual listening pulse.
- The spider can return to Dormant after the player becomes distant and quiet.

### Perception
Player movement is converted into a lightweight noise value:
- idle = 0
- walking = 0.32
- sprinting = 1.0

Perception is distance-based and deterministic.

### Intentional limits
v0.5 does **not** include:
- chasing
- attacking
- damage
- death
- hiding interaction
- screech events

Those belong to later locked milestones.

### Performance
- One spider entity is active.
- No physics engine is introduced.
- No expensive pathfinding is active.
- Rendering uses lightweight Canvas primitives.
