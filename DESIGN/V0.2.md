# The Castle Spider — v0.2
## PLAYER

### Goal
Replace the foundation marker with a real player-controller architecture that can become the final knight without rewriting the movement system.

### Systems
- Player entity
- Horizontal and vertical movement foundation
- Acceleration/deceleration
- Walking
- Sprinting
- Stamina drain
- Stamina recovery
- Facing direction
- World bounds
- Player bounds
- Player rendering placeholder
- Player scene
- HUD foundation

### Design constraints
- Movement must feel deliberate rather than instant.
- Sprinting must have a meaningful tradeoff.
- Stamina must recover predictably.
- The player must never move outside the test world.
- Rendering must remain lightweight.
- The temporary player art is replaceable.

### Controls
- A / Left Arrow: left
- D / Right Arrow: right
- W / Up Arrow: up
- S / Down Arrow: down
- Shift: sprint

### v0.2 acceptance criteria
1. Player can move.
2. Player has acceleration/deceleration.
3. Sprint works.
4. Stamina drains during sprint.
5. Stamina recovers when not sprinting.
6. Player remains inside world bounds.
7. Player scene is separate from engine/core code.
8. Player state is exposed cleanly for later animation and interaction systems.
