# The Castle Spider — v0
## LOCKED GAME DESIGN

v0 is the game's design contract. It defines what we build toward before production systems are expanded.

---

## 1. GAME IDENTITY

**Title:** The Castle Spider  
**Genre:** 2D side-view survival horror  
**Primary platform:** Browser  
**Priority hardware:** Low-spec browser devices  
**Camera:** Side-on, exploration-focused  
**Core threat:** A giant spider-like creature  
**Player fantasy:** You are vulnerable, observant, and resourceful—not an unstoppable fighter.

### Tone
Dark, tense, mysterious, atmospheric, and occasionally quiet.

The game should create fear through anticipation and uncertainty rather than nonstop attacks.

---

## 2. DESIGN PILLARS

### Tension
The player should frequently suspect danger before danger is visible.

### Risk
Moving quickly, exploring further, or making noise can provide progress while increasing danger.

### Uncertainty
Encounters, sounds, and some environmental events should not always happen the same way.

### Progression
The player becomes more capable over time, but later sections introduce stronger challenges.

---

## 3. PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Most of the game is:

**Explore → observe → listen → make a decision → move → react to danger → survive → continue**

The player is not expected to fight constantly.

The central question during an encounter is:

> **"Where can I survive this?"**

---

## 4. THE SPIDER

The spider is not merely a moving obstacle.

It will eventually use a state-based AI:

**Dormant → Listening → Suspicious → Searching → Hunting → Chasing → Lost**

Its behavior can be influenced by:

- Player movement
- Sound
- Visibility
- Environment
- Encounter progression

### Important rule
The spider must feel dangerous without feeling random or unfair.

The player should be able to learn its behavior.

---

## 5. THE CASTLE

The castle is a major part of the game, not just a background.

It will eventually contain:

- Main halls
- Side corridors
- Rooms
- Locked areas
- Secret routes
- Environmental storytelling
- Major set pieces
- Spider-controlled areas

The layout should support multiple ways of moving through the castle rather than being one uninterrupted hallway.

---

## 6. STORY FRAMEWORK

The player enters an abandoned castle connected to a disturbing history.

The complete truth is deliberately not given immediately.

The story is revealed through:

- Environment
- Objects
- Notes
- Rooms
- Dialogue where useful
- Optional discoveries
- Major events

### Story principle
The player should understand the mystery gradually.

We will avoid dumping the entire story into an opening cutscene.

---

## 7. VISUAL DIRECTION

The final game should have a distinctive 2D visual identity.

Priorities:

1. Readable silhouettes
2. Strong lighting contrast
3. Detailed environments without unnecessary rendering complexity
4. Distinct character silhouettes
5. Atmospheric backgrounds
6. Clear interaction points

Placeholder art is acceptable during engineering, but it will not be treated as final art.

---

## 8. AUDIO DIRECTION

Audio is a major gameplay system.

Important categories:

- Footsteps
- Castle ambience
- Wind
- Doors
- Environmental noises
- Distant sounds
- Spider movement
- Spider warnings/screeches
- Chase audio
- UI sounds
- Music used selectively

### Audio principle
Silence is allowed to exist.

The game should not constantly tell the player how to feel with music.

---

## 9. CONTROLS

Initial browser controls:

- **A / Left Arrow:** move left
- **D / Right Arrow:** move right
- **Shift:** sprint
- **W / Up Arrow:** interact/hide where appropriate
- **E:** context interaction when needed later
- **Escape:** pause

Controls must remain keyboard-friendly.

---

## 10. PERFORMANCE CONTRACT

Performance is designed in from the beginning.

The game must prefer:

- Lightweight rendering
- Efficient collision
- Limited active objects
- Camera/world culling
- Reusable objects where useful
- Controlled effects
- Minimal unnecessary per-frame work

A fancy feature that seriously harms playability on the target hardware can be postponed or redesigned.

---

## 11. FIRST PLAYABLE SLICE — v0.1

The first proper build is intentionally small.

It must demonstrate the game's core identity with:

- A functioning game shell
- One castle environment
- Player movement
- Camera following
- Sprint + stamina
- Basic interaction
- At least one hiding method
- One spider encounter
- Warning/screech system
- Spider pursuit
- Survival outcome
- A clear escape/end point
- Basic restart flow

### v0.1 does NOT require

- Full castle
- Full story
- Upgrade system
- Complex inventory
- Multiple enemy types
- Procedural generation
- Multiple endings
- Full save system
- Final art
- Final audio
- Large content catalog

The purpose of v0.1 is to prove that **The Castle Spider is fun and tense at its core.**

---

## 12. WHAT WE ARE NOT BUILDING YET

To prevent scope explosion, these stay postponed until their roadmap phase:

- Large progression systems
- Complex puzzles
- Huge procedural worlds
- Multiple major enemies
- Full replayability systems
- Final production art
- Final audio library
- Advanced save architecture
- Release packaging

---

## 13. v0 ACCEPTANCE TEST

v0 is considered complete when we can clearly answer:

**What is The Castle Spider?**  
A 2D survival-horror exploration game centered on tension, sound, hiding, and escaping a dangerous castle.

**What does the player do?**  
Explore, listen, make risk decisions, survive encounters, discover information, and progress toward escape.

**Why is the spider scary?**  
Its presence is communicated through sound, uncertainty, searching, and pursuit rather than constant scripted attacks.

**Why explore the castle?**  
Exploration reveals routes, resources, secrets, story information, and progression.

**What changes as the game progresses?**  
The player's capabilities increase while encounters and environments become more demanding.

**What is the first real milestone?**  
A small, polished vertical slice proving the core exploration/hiding/spider loop.
