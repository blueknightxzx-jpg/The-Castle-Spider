# The Castle Spider — v0
## LOCKED GAME DESIGN

v0 is the game's design contract.

## 1. GAME IDENTITY
**Title:** The Castle Spider
**Genre:** 2D side-view survival horror
**Primary platform:** Browser
**Priority hardware:** Low-spec browser devices
**Camera:** Side-on
**Core threat:** A giant spider-like creature
**Starting character:** Neutral Default character; additional skins are future content
**Primary traversal model:** Horizontal castle-hallway exploration

### Tone
Dark, tense, mysterious, atmospheric, and occasionally quiet.

## 2. DESIGN PILLARS
### Tension
Danger can be sensed before it is seen.

### Risk
Speed, movement, exploration and later interaction can carry consequences.

### Uncertainty
Encounters, sounds and environmental events should not always repeat exactly.

### Progression
The player becomes more capable while later sections become more demanding.

## 3. PLAYER EXPERIENCE
Most of the game is:
**Explore → observe → listen → decide → move → react → survive → continue**

The player is not expected to constantly fight.

Central question:
> **"Where can I survive this?"**

## 4. THE SPIDER
The spider will eventually use:
**Dormant → Listening → Suspicious → Searching → Hunting → Chasing → Lost**

Its behavior may react to movement, sound, visibility and the environment.

## 5. THE CASTLE
The castle is a major gameplay space.

It is primarily traversed as connected, side-view hallways with:
- architectural landmarks
- doors and future interaction points
- hiding areas
- environmental storytelling
- secrets
- major set pieces
- spider-controlled areas

The castle should feel deliberately constructed rather than like a collection of unrelated background rectangles.

## 6. STORY FRAMEWORK
The player enters an abandoned castle connected to a disturbing history. The truth is discovered gradually through the environment, objects, notes, dialogue where useful, optional discoveries and major events.

## 7. VISUAL DIRECTION
Priorities:
1. Readable silhouettes
2. Strong lighting contrast
3. Structured architecture
4. Distinct character silhouettes
5. Atmospheric backgrounds
6. Clear interaction points
7. Deliberate alignment and spacing

Placeholder art is replaceable.

## 8. AUDIO DIRECTION
Important categories:
- footsteps
- castle ambience
- wind
- doors
- environmental noises
- distant sounds
- spider movement
- warnings/screeches
- chase audio
- UI sounds
- selective music

Silence is allowed to exist.

## 9. CONTROLS
- **A / Left Arrow:** move left
- **D / Right Arrow:** move right
- **Shift:** sprint
- **E:** reserved interaction key; functionality begins later
- **Escape:** future pause control

**W / S / Up / Down:** deliberately unused for player movement.

## 10. PERFORMANCE CONTRACT
Prefer lightweight rendering, efficient collision, limited active objects, camera/world culling, reusable objects, controlled effects and minimal unnecessary per-frame work.

## 11. FIRST PLAYABLE SLICE
The first meaningful playable build must eventually demonstrate:
- game shell
- castle hallway
- player movement
- camera following
- sprint + stamina
- interaction
- hiding
- spider encounter
- warning/screech
- pursuit
- survival outcome
- escape/end point
- restart

## 12. CURRENT STRUCTURAL RULE
The player controller must be horizontal-first and appearance-independent. Future skins attach to the player rather than changing its movement architecture.
