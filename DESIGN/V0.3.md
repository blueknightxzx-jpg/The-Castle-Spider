# The Castle Spider — v0.3
## CASTLE

### Goal
Replace the isolated player test room with a reusable castle-world foundation large enough to support exploration and later horror systems.

### Systems
- Castle world data
- Multiple connected castle sections
- Long side-view traversal space
- World-sized camera travel
- Architectural rendering
- Windows
- Pillars
- Floor structure
- Torches / light zones
- North exit marker
- Castle scene
- Player integrated into the castle world

### Design constraints
- The castle must feel like a place, not a repeating rectangle.
- Sections should have visual identities without requiring expensive rendering.
- The world must be larger than the viewport.
- The camera must move smoothly through the world.
- Architecture must be data-driven enough to expand later.
- v0.3 does not implement full interaction or final assets.

### Acceptance criteria
1. Player can traverse a multi-section castle.
2. Camera follows player through the world.
3. Castle architecture changes between sections.
4. World boundaries are enforced.
5. Castle data is separated from renderer code.
6. Rendering remains lightweight.
7. Player system remains reusable.
